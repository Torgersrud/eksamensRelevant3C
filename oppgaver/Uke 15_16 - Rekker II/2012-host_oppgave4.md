a) Use Newton's method on the function $f(x) = x^2 - 3$ to show that $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$ with three decimal places of accuracy. Use $x_0 = 2$ as the starting value.

b) Show that

$$\arctan(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{x^{2n+1}}{2n+1}$$

for $|x| < 1$.

[Hint: Integrate an appropriate geometric series from $0$ to $x$.]

c) Use the result from **b)** to compute $\pi$ with an error less than $2 \cdot 10^{-3}$.

[*Hint*: Recall that $\frac{\pi}{6} = \arctan \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$. Use the approximation $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$ from **a**).]
