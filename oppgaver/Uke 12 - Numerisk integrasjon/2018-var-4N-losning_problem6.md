#### Problem 6

a) For a given function f(t), give an expression for the polynomial of lowest possible degree interpolating the function in the nodes $t_0 = -1/3$ and $t_1 = 1$ . Use the result to find a quadrature rule $Q[-1,1] = w_0 f(t_0) + w_1 f(t_1)$ as an approximation to the integral $\int_{-1}^{1} f(t) dt$.

Find the degree of precision of the quadrature rule.

**b)** Transfer the quadrature rule Q[-1,1] to some arbitrary interval [a,b]. Use it to find an approximation of the integral $\int_1^2 x^2 \sin(\pi x/2) dx$.
