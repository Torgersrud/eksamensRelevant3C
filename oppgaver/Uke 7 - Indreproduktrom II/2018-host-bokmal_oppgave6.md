## Oppgave 6

$$\mathbf{v}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 2\\1\\1\\0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{v}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1\\0\\-2\\1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{v}_3 = \begin{bmatrix} 1\\1\\1\\0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{v}_4 = \begin{bmatrix} 2\\1\\-1\\1 \end{bmatrix}$$
